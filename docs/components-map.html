Sprinto Outreach · Components Map

Paste a company's domain: the pipeline reads its site, checks the fit for Sprinto, finds the right person and drafts a cold email in about a minute. Gemini Flash writes the words; plain code decides.

The Cut

The AI never decides who gets an email. Gemini fills in facts, quotes the page and writes the words; plain code applies the Evidence Map, the fit bar and the contact ranking, and checks every draft. Nothing reaches a prospect: each email lands in Sharan's own inbox first.

Humans stay here

  • Sharan picks which companies to research
  • He reads and edits every draft before it goes out
  • Every send goes to his own inbox, never to the prospect
  • A guessed email address is flagged for him to check

When something fails

  • Not B2B SaaS? Stops after the homepage, no contact search
  • Model rate limit? Waits and retries up to 4×
  • No confident contact? Marked "needs a contact" for a person
  • Draft fails a check? Redrafted once, then flagged
